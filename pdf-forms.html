How to use

  1. Click Upload PDF.
  2. Select Text Field, Checkbox, Radio Button or Dropdown.
  3. Enter the Field Name and optional Default Value.
  4. Click the PDF preview where you want the field.
  5. Drag the field to move it.
  6. Drag the red circle to resize it.
  7. Select a field and use Duplicate or Delete.
  8. Click Create Fillable PDF.
  9. Download and open the generated PDF in a PDF reader to test the fields.
