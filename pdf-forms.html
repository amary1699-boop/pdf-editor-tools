How to use

  1. Click Upload PDF and select your PDF.
  2. Select Text Field, Checkbox, Radio Button or Dropdown.
  3. Enter Field Name and Default Value.
  4. Click the PDF preview to add the field.
  5. Drag the field to change its position.
  6. Use the small red circle to resize it.
  7. Select a field and use Duplicate or Delete.
  8. Click Create Fillable PDF.
  9. Download the generated PDF.
